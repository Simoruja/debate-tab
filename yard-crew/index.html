Hi, neighbor

We'd rather be raking your leaves than sitting on the couch.

We started Leaves Away because every fall we'd walk past yards buried in leaves and think, we could do that, and do it well. We show up when we say we will, we bag every last leaf, and we text you a photo when we're done. You get your Saturday back.

  • Fair, upfront prices. You see the price before we start, and it never goes up without your OK.
  • The same faces every time. No random subcontractors. It's us.
  • Pay after. Venmo, Zelle or cash once you're happy with the job.
